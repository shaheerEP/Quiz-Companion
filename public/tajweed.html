Makhraj of Hamzah (ء / أ)

Deepest throat right above the chest. For Sukoon (أَءْ), both vocal cords snap 100% shut, stopping all air and sound completely!

Try It With 2 Fingers! (Feel It On Neck)

Place 2 fingers in the soft hollow pit right above your collarbone. Say "أَءْ" — feel the sudden firm stop inside your voice box!

Mechanical Articulation Controls (التحكم بالمخرج)

1. Characteristics with Opposites (ٱلصِّفَاتُ الْمُتَضَادَّةُ — 4 Pairs)

4 Opposite Pairs (9 Sifaat)
1

1. Flow of Breath

Whether breath continues or is stopped during pronunciation.
هَمْسWhispering / Concealment

Continuation of the breath due to weak reliance on the articulation point.

Mnemonic:(فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتَ)
جَهْرClarity / Loudness

Stopping the flow of breath due to strong reliance on the articulation point (Makhraj).

2

2. Flow of Sound

Degree of sound flow: stopping, moderate, or continuing.
شِدَّةStrength

Stopping the flow of sound due to strong, firm reliance on the articulation point.

Mnemonic:(أَجِدُ قَطٍ بَكَتْ)
تَوَسُّطModeration

Moderate flow of sound between softness and strength.

Mnemonic:(لِنْ عُمَرْ)
رِخْوSoftness / Looseness

Easy flow of sound due to weak reliance on the articulation point.

3

3. Elevation & Lowering — Root of Tongue

Focus: Root of Tongue — Raising the root towards the upper palate vs Lowering it to mouth floor.
اِسْتِعْلَاءElevation
👅Root of Tongue (أقصى اللسان)

Raising the root / back of the tongue (أقصى اللسان) towards the upper palate, directing sound upwards (Tafkheem).

Mnemonic:(خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ)
اِسْتِفَالLowering
👅Root of Tongue (أقصى اللسان)

Lowering the root of the tongue (أقصى اللسان) to the mouth floor away from the palate, keeping sound flat (Tarqeeq).

4

4. Adhesion & Opening — Middle of Tongue

Focus: Middle of Tongue — Adhering the middle of the tongue to the upper palate vs Separating it.
إِطْبَاقAdhesion / Closing
👅Middle of Tongue (وسط اللسان)

Adhering the middle of the tongue (وسط اللسان) to the upper palate, trapping and confining sound (strongest heaviness).

اِنْفِتَاحOpening / Separation
👅Middle of Tongue (وسط اللسان)

Keeping the middle of the tongue (وسط اللسان) separated from the upper palate, allowing sound to escape unconfined.

2. Characteristics without Opposites (ٱلصِّفَاتُ الَّتِى لَيْسَ لَهَا الْأَضْدَادُ — 7 Sifaat)

7 Individual Sifaat
صَفِيرBird's chirp / Whistle
👅Tip of Tongue (طرف اللسان)

A sharp sound resembling the chirping of a bird.

قَلْقَلَةShaking / Echoing

An echoing or vibrating sound produced by pressing firmly on the articulation point.

Mnemonic:(قُطْبُ جَدٍّ)
لِينGentleness / Ease

Pronouncing the letter effortlessly without any strain on the tongue (when saakin preceded by fathah).

Mnemonic:(الواو والياء الساكنتان المفتوح ما قبلهما)
اِنْحِرَافInclination / Drifting
👅Tongue Tip & Sides (طرف وحافتي اللسان)

Drifting from its own articulation point to another (ر inclines towards ل, and ل inclines towards ض).

تَكْرِيرRepetition / Trilling
👅Tip of Tongue (طرف اللسان)

Shivering or trilling of the tip of the tongue (this trait is taught so that excessive trilling is avoided).

تَفَشِّيSpreading
👅Middle of Tongue (وسط اللسان)

Spreading of breath inside the mouth until it reaches the articulation point of ظ.

اِسْتِطَالَةElongation
👅Side of Tongue (حافة اللسان)

Stretching of the sound along the entire length of the side of the tongue.
